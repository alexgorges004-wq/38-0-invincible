# ⚽ 38-0: Invincible

**Build the ultimate historical football team and achieve perfection.**

A viral browser-based football draft simulator where you assemble an 11-man squad from legendary players across different eras and attempt to win all 38 games undefeated.

## 🎮 Features

### Core Gameplay
- **Draft Mechanics**: Spin a wheel to get a random team + era, then pick players
- **Tactical Formations**: Choose from 4-3-3, 4-4-2, or 3-4-3 setups
- **Squad Building**: Drag-and-drop players onto the pitch
- **Chemistry System**: Bonus stats for players from same club/nation/league
- **Season Simulation**: See if your squad can go 38-0

### Game Modes
- **Classic**: All player stats and OVR visible
- **Football IQ**: Blind draft - stats hidden, rely on knowledge

### Draft Pools
- Global (all players)
- Premier League
- La Liga
- Serie A
- Bundesliga

## 🎮 How to Play

1. **Select Draft Pool**: Choose Global, Premier League, La Liga, or Serie A
2. **Choose Game Mode**: Classic (visible stats) or Football IQ (blind)
3. **Pick Formation**: 4-3-3, 4-4-2, or 3-4-3
4. **Start Draft**: Press "Enter Draft"
5. **Roll for Team/Era**: Spin the slot machine to get a random team and decade
6. **Pick Players**: Select 11 players from the available pool
7. **Place on Pitch**: Drag players to eligible positions on the tactical pitch
8. **Simulate Season**: See if your team can achieve 38-0
